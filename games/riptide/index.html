RIPTIDE

You're stranded in open water. Reach the shore before panic takes you.

"Sometimes the safest move is to stop swimming."

Aim + Stroke — move your pointer/finger toward where you want to go, then click/tap (or press Space) to stroke. Each stroke costs stamina.
Stamina — regenerates only while you're not stroking. Run it to zero and you can't fight the water at all.
Panic — falls while you're rested, creeps up as stamina gets low, and spikes once you're exhausted. Panic reaching 100% ends the swim.
The water — currents, a whirlpool, jellyfish, and boat wakes all push you around whether you're moving or not. Sometimes drifting is safer than fighting.
