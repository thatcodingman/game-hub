QUICK DRAW

You're in a stand-off. Your opponent will twitch, flinch, and bluff, trying to bait you into drawing early. Only one signal is real.

"Watch for the flash. Ignore everything else."

Feints — hand twitches, dust puffs, crowd gasps. These are NOT the signal. Click during one and you flinch — game over.
The Draw — a bright white flash across the screen with a bell tone. That's the only real cue. React as fast as you can.
Too slow — miss the window after the real draw and your opponent gets the drop on you.
Every round you survive, the feints get faster, closer together, and start looking a lot more like the real thing — a half-draw, a duller flash, a distant shot. Watch for the exact flash and tone, not just any movement or bang.
