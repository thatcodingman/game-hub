ABS CHALLENGES
PITCHING — pick a pitch, press and hold on the zone to aim. The circle shrinks to a sweet spot (~0.9s) then grows; release when it is tightest. Tired arms lose command.
BATTING — move the bat ring over where you expect the ball, tap to swing. Early pulls, late goes the other way. Low on the ball lifts it.
RUNNING — hit STEAL, then tap GO when the pitcher's leg lifts. On defense a long hold lets runners run: quick pitches and PICKOFF hold them.
ABS — after a called ball/strike you can CHALLENGE. Win = keep it. Lose = it is gone. Extra innings start with a runner on 2nd.
PITCH CLOCK — set it in the menu (off, 10, 15 MLB, 20 or 30 seconds; +3 with runners on). Pitcher not into his motion in time = automatic ball.
DERBY — pick a slugger, 2:00 on the clock, grooved BP fastballs. A 440-footer earns 30 bonus seconds.
SEASON — 14 games, standings, top four make the playoffs. Saved on this device.
