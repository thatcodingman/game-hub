CONTROLS

A / D move your puck

Q / E adjust the angle

Hold Space to charge, release to shoot

Or drag back anywhere on the board with the mouse and let go

Esc or P pauses

SCORING

Zone 3, 2 and 1 are worth 3, 2 and 1 points.

Hang a puck over the end edge for 4.

Only the side with the furthest puck scores that frame.

Pucks that stop short of the foul line are removed.

First to 21 wins.
